Din guzarte hain, jaise raaste ghis jaate hon<br>
Har mod par wahi purani khamoshi,<br>
Aur hum, be-naam safar ke musafir

Zindagi palat kar kabhi dekh'ti nahin,<br>
Jo kho gaya, woh bas hawa ki dhool ban jaata hai<br>
Na roshni rok paati hai, na dua

Boondh gir kar mitti mein ghul jaati hai,<br>
Nadiyaa samundar tak laut'ti hain,<br>
Magar insaan ka raasta ek hi simt mein likha hai

Waqt, ek be-reham darindaa,<br>
Jo yaadon ko chaba kar khaali kar deta hai<br>
Aur hum apne saaye ke saath<br>
Ek anjaan shaam ki taraf kheench'te chale jaate hain

Sapne adhoore, mohabbat aadhi,<br>
Batein adhuri reh jaati hain.<br>
Aur dil ke kisi kone mein bas jaam jaati hai<br>
Ek chhupi hui kasak
