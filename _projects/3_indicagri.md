**Top 14 Nationals** – [Capital One Launchpad Hackathon](https://capitalone.hackerearth.com/) (Aug 2025)

- Developed multi-modal Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) chatbot supporting **20+** Indian languages for agricultural advisory services.
- Engineered autonomous data pipeline constructing 15,000+ document knowledge base from government agricultural resources; implemented chunking strategies and embedding optimization for domain-specific retrieval.
- Achieved **45%** reduction in inference latency through model quantization (INT8/INT4), KV-cache optimization, and intelligent response caching mechanisms.
